Geometric Intuition

Wedge products emphasize antisymmetric contrast and edge-like relations; inner products emphasize symmetric alignment and coherence.

Backward Summary

Backward unrolls activation, residual/drop-path/layer-scale, higher-order and global paths, then distributes wedge and inner gradients back through the cyclic channel schedules.
